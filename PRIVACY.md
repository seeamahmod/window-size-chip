# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Window Size Chip is a browser extension that shows your window and viewport size and lets you view pages at other screen sizes.

## What it collects

**Nothing.** The extension does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, browsing history, page content or usage data. It has no server and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else.

## What stays on your computer

- Your **Vertical / Horizontal** choice, saved in your browser's extension storage.
- While you use a tablet or phone size: the address of the page you're viewing (so **Reset** can take you back) and the page's background color. These are kept only in the browser's temporary session storage and are cleared when you press Reset, leave the page, close the tab or close the browser.

## How permissions are used

| Permission | Used for |
|---|---|
| Tabs | Reading the current tab's address and resizing its window. |
| Storage | Remembering your Vertical / Horizontal choice. |
| Scripting | Loading a small helper into pages only while a tablet or phone size is showing, and into already-open tabs after an update. |
| Read and change data on all sites | Measuring the page's viewport size, taking a screenshot of the visible tab to pick a matching background color (the image never leaves your browser and isn't saved), and showing the page at tablet and phone sizes. |
| Network request rules | Only for the tab showing a tablet or phone size: sending that device's browser identity and letting the page load inside the extension's view. Removed as soon as you press Reset or leave. |

## Third parties

None. The extension contains no analytics, ads or third-party code.
